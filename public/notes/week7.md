Overview of the Week

Summary  
After the recent competition we have decided that one part of our team will be reconstructing the drivetrain to make it faster using the blue motors and the other part of the team will be working on building a cascade/continous.

Drive train  
For the gear ratios we were going to switch up the gear placement so that we could maximize speed with our green motors but we acquired 4 blue motors and decided to use those instead for the obvious reason that blue motors can go max 600 rpm and green motors can go max 400rpm. We did a 1:3 gear ratio and the image to the left is a picture of one side of the drivetrain. Instead of changing the spacing of the wheels and putting in multiple spacer, we used shaft collars instead.

The picture above shows the side angled view fo the drivetrain. Our drivetrain can now reach the max speed of 450 rpm. The only issue is that this number will probably decrease as the lift gets added on.
